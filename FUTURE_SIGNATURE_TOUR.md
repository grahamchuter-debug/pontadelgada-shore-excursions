# Signature São Miguel Experience — Internal Planning

**Working title:** Signature São Miguel Discovery
**Status:** In preparation only — not a live or bookable product
**Public placeholder:** `/signature-sao-miguel-experience`
**Editorial rule:** Never imply that the experience, inclusions, price or departure dates are confirmed. Never imply a specific crater view or wildlife sighting is guaranteed.

## Product idea

Create a premium, Sete Cidades-focused shore experience from Ponta Delgada for no more than eight guests. The day should combine São Miguel's signature crater-lake view with weather-aware timing, rather than trying to cover every landscape on the island.

The concept is intentionally different from a standard coach tour:

- maximum eight guests
- comfortable, modern vehicle suited to crater roads
- cruise-terminal collection and return at Portas do Mar
- Sete Cidades as the clear anchor
- flexible stop order to make the most of the morning's weather
- a relaxed local lunch
- itinerary flexibility within a conservative ship schedule
- an excellent English-speaking local guide

Until suppliers, route testing and commercial terms are complete, all public copy must use language such as "in preparation", "planned" and "future".

## Intended passenger

- First-time or repeat Ponta Delgada cruise passenger seeking São Miguel's volcanic landscapes
- Values scenery, local context, food and photography over a checklist
- Wants a smaller group than a conventional coach excursion
- Comfortable with changeable weather and some walking at viewpoints
- Premium or mid-premium budget

This is not intended for passengers whose priority is a guaranteed clear crater view, anyone needing step-free access throughout, or calls without enough time and return margin.

## Indicative route

The final sequence must be tested against real ship calls. A possible structure for a half-to-full-day call:

1. **Ponta Delgada terminal collection** — named meeting point at Portas do Mar and direct day-of contact
2. **Early departure inland** — guide introduction, timed to beat crater-rim cloud where possible
3. **Sete Cidades viewpoints** — Vista do Rei and the Boca do Inferno viewpoint before peak visitor numbers
4. **Caldera village discovery** — history, lanes and a manageable walking route around the village and lake edge
5. **Local lunch** — pre-vetted restaurant with dietary arrangements
6. **Photography stop** — a second viewpoint selected for light and conditions on the day
7. **Direct return to Ponta Delgada** — timed backwards from all-aboard with a documented contingency

The launch itinerary should omit any stop that weakens the Sete Cidades experience or erodes the return buffer.

## Experience standards

### Group and vehicle

- Hard cap of eight paying guests
- Modern, comfortable vehicle suited to crater roads
- Passenger and luggage capacity checked at full group size
- Seat allocation and child-seat policy confirmed before sale

### Guide

- Fluent, natural English
- Strong São Miguel geology, history and Azorean cultural knowledge
- Comfortable adapting commentary and route order to weather on the day
- Proven Ponta Delgada cruise-port experience

### Food

- Lunch venue tested for quality, service time and group handling
- Vegetarian and common dietary requirements supported with notice
- Lunch must feel local without becoming a timing risk
- No unconfirmed meal inclusion or sample menu in public sales copy

### Cruise timing

- Route planned from all-aboard, not ship departure
- Separate timing models for shorter and longer calls
- Weather contingency for crater-rim cloud and road conditions
- Named fallback route (for example, a lower-altitude alternative) when conditions make the full plan unsafe or pointless
- Day-of ship and berth monitoring assigned to the operating partner

## Partner requirements

Before launch, the partner must provide:

- licences, insurance and vehicle documentation
- written cancellation and missed-port terms
- emergency and breakdown procedures
- guide and driver quality standards
- child-seat and accessibility policies
- restaurant arrangements and dietary process
- clear commission, payment and refund terms
- evidence of reliable cruise-passenger returns

## Validation plan

1. Run the complete route privately under realistic weekday conditions.
2. Repeat during a period of poor crater-rim weather to test the fallback plan.
3. Record drive, viewpoint, lunch and comfort-stop timings.
4. Test the route with eight occupied passenger seats.
5. Review walking demands and the accuracy of accessibility copy.
6. Photograph only what a normal departure can reliably deliver — including on a cloudy day.
7. Obtain structured feedback from trial guests.
8. Launch only when the editorial and operations teams approve the return margins.
